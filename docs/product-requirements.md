# Product requirements

## Objective

Give pharmacy staff a dependable mobile workflow for knowing what stock is available, recording
stock movement, and verifying AI-assisted shelf capture.

## Users

- Owner: manages pharmacy settings and users.
- Manager: manages inventory and reports.
- Pharmacist: records dispensing and reviews AI captures.
- Stock clerk: performs counts and stock adjustments.

## MVP acceptance criteria

- A staff member can create an inventory item.
- A staff member can add or remove stock with a reason.
- The inventory list shows current quantity and stock status.
- Low-stock items can be filtered.
- A shelf image can be uploaded to private storage.
- AI suggestions are stored as pending until reviewed.
- Each AI suggestion can be accepted, edited, or rejected.
- Manual entry remains available when image capture fails.
- Stock changes retain the actor, time, source, and reason.
